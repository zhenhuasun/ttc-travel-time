Toronto by subway

How long does it really take to cross Toronto by subway and tram? Set a start point: the whole city is colored by travel time, computed from the TTC network's official schedules.

Open the Toronto travel-time map →

How it works

  1. 1. Pick a start An address, a station, or your location: the map colors around it.
  2. 2. Click a destination You get the duration and the itinerary: which line to take, transfers, walking.
  3. 3. Compare The 15- and 30-minute curves show what is really close, by subway, tram, or bus.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the travel times come from?

From the official scheduled timetables of the TTC network, published as open data in GTFS format. They cover an ordinary weekday, between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m.

Are the displayed times reliable?

These are "on paper" averages: walk to the stop, waiting time equal to half the interval between two services, scheduled time between stops, and transfers. No real-time data or disruptions.

Are buses included?

Yes, optionally on the map. By default, only subways and trams are shown, to highlight the backbone of the network.
