      Frequently asked questions

      How long does it take to cross Clermont-Ferrand by tram?

      From downtown (Place de Jaude), 41% of tram stations are within 15 minutes and 94% within 30 minutes, walking and waiting included. The farthest, Les Vergnes, is about 34 minutes away.

      How frequent are the tram lines in Clermont-Ferrand?

      On a weekday during the day, the average interval between two services is tram A: 5.9 min. The most frequent line is A, with a service about every 5.9 minutes.

      Where do the timetables used come from?

      From the scheduled timetables published by the T2C network (GTFS format, Licence Ouverte 2.0), downloaded on 4 October 2026 and valid until 31 December 2026. Travel times are for Tuesday 6 October 2026, between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m.

      Are buses included in Clermont-Ferrand?

      Yes, optionally: check "Bus" under the map. By default, only tram are shown. Waiting time at infrequent bus stops is capped at 15 minutes.

      How are travel times calculated?

      For each trip: walk to the stop at 4.5 km/h, waiting time equal to half the interval between two services, scheduled time between stops, transfers with 1.5 minutes of walking. No real-time data or disruptions: this is the city "on paper".

      Who made this map of Clermont-Ferrand?

      The idea comes from NYC Transit Time Cartogram by Anthony Castrio, later adapted to Paris by Jules Grandin (C'est encore loin ?). These maps are made by Camille Roux, developer and co-founder of Human Coders in Montpellier: see his other projects and his weekly tech watch. The code is open on GitHub.
