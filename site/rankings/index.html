Method

Each ranking details its own measurement. These figures involve no trip computation: they are direct counts from the scheduled timetables published by each network (GTFS), for a Tuesday or Thursday during the school term. Only trams and subways are counted (no buses, busways, funiculars or cable cars); Rhônexpress and the OL Stadium shuttle are excluded in Lyon. In Paris, these figures count the Île-de-France Mobilités metro and tram, excluding RER, Transilien, CDGVAL, Orlyval and the Montmartre funicular. Days used: Angers (3 November 2026), Besançon (6 October 2026), Bordeaux (5 November 2026), Brest (13 October 2026), Clermont-Ferrand (6 October 2026), Dijon (6 October 2026), Grenoble (3 November 2026), Le Mans (6 October 2026), Lille (3 November 2026), Lyon (6 October 2026), Marseille (3 November 2026), Montpellier (3 November 2026), Nantes (3 November 2026), Nice (3 November 2026), Orléans (3 November 2026), Paris (6 October 2026), Reims (6 October 2026), Rennes (6 October 2026), Rouen (6 October 2026), Saint-Étienne (6 October 2026), Strasbourg (3 November 2026), Toulouse (6 October 2026), Tours (6 October 2026).
